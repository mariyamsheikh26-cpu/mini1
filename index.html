Masala Maggi

Quick, tasty and easy recipe!

About This Recipe

Masala Maggi is a delicious and easy snack.

Preparation Time: 5 minutes

Cooking Time: 5 minutes

Ingredients

  • 1 packet Maggi noodles
  • 2 cups water
  • 1 chopped onion
  • 1 chopped tomato
  • 1 packet Maggi masala
  • 1 spoon oil

👩‍🍳 How to Make

  1. Heat oil in a pan.
  2. Add chopped onion and tomato.
  3. Add water and boil it.
  4. Add noodles and Maggi masala.
  5. Cook for 2–3 minutes.
  6. Your tasty Maggi is ready!

💡 Cooking Tips

  • Add vegetables for extra taste.
  • Do not add too much water.
  • Serve hot and enjoy!
